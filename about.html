About Me

A little bit about who I am

Hello! I'm Arnav S., an aspiring full-stack developer. I was born in Virginia, USA and lived there until I was 10. My family then moved to India to be closer to cousins and grandparents — and though we eventually had to move back, that time changed everything. My cousin introduced me to Scratch, and that small moment sparked a passion for building things with code that's never stopped growing.
